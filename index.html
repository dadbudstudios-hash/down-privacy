Privacy Policy for D.O.W.N.

Developer: DadBud Studios
Contact: dadbudstudios@gmail.com
Last updated: 13 November 2026

D.O.W.N. ("the game") is an endless-falling arcade game for Android. This policy explains what information the game and its partners handle.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (scores, coins, unlocked items, settings, missions and achievements) is saved in a file on your device. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded by us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, in order to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you buy something in the game, the payment is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. We receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can unlock it.

4. Network requests for the date and time

To prevent clock tampering in daily and weekly rewards, the game contacts well-known public websites over HTTPS to read the current date. Those sites can see your IP address, as with any web request. The game sends no personal data in these requests.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear app data. We do not keep your game data on our servers. To ask about any data, email us at the address above.

7. Security

We use reputable services (Google Play, Google AdMob) and keep data collection to the minimum the game needs.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: dadbudstudios@gmail.com
